# Adversarial Tradecraft — Blue Team

The defensive (blue-team) content pulled out of my **CIS4930 Cyber Adversarial Tradecraft** class. The course is mostly offensive; these are the sections that are about *detecting and responding* to an attacker, framed for CCDC-style competition defense.

## Sections
1. [Preparing for Battle](./01-preparing-for-battle.md) — planning, runbooks, defense in depth, telemetry categories.
2. [Logging & SIEM](./02-logging-and-siem.md) — log types, event types to watch, syslog, Sysmon, Zeek, SIEM/Wazuh.
3. [Detection Tools](./03-detection-tools.md) — EDR, and IDS/IPS (Snort rules, Suricata).
4. [Analysis Tools](./04-analysis-tools.md) — Sysinternals, YARA, Binwalk, malware sandboxes.
